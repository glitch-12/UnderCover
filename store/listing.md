## Full description (4000 char max)

Gather your friends around one phone and find out who's lying.

UNDERCOVER is a pass-and-play social deduction party game for 3–20 players. Everyone gets a secret word — except the Undercover players get a word that's almost the same, but not quite. Give one-word clues, listen closely, and vote out whoever sounds suspicious before they vote you out first.

No sign-up. No internet required. No account needed. Just hand the phone around and play.

HOW TO PLAY
• Add your players and pass the device around for a private, one-tap role reveal
• Take turns giving a single-word clue about your secret word — no repeats
• Discuss, then vote to eliminate whoever seems Undercover
• Civilians win by rooting out every Undercover player. Undercover wins by blending in and outlasting the group

THREE WAYS TO PLAY
• Classic — one Undercover hiding among the Civilians
• Mr. White — a wildcard player with no word at all, who can steal the win with one clever guess
• Multi-Undercover — the number of Undercover players scales up with your table size, perfect for bigger groups

BUILT FOR PARTY NIGHT
• Fast round setup — get playing in under a minute
• Scales from a small game night (3 players) up to a full house (20 players)
• Clean, distraction-free dark-themed interface designed for passing a phone hand to hand
• Every round is quick, so you can play again immediately

Perfect for house parties, road trips, family game night, icebreakers, and any time you've got a group of people and one phone.

Undercover is free to play.
